Who we are

Based in Thessaloniki (Head Office) and Athens (Branch Office), IMEMA distributes medical equipment from leading foreign manufacturers to Greek private and public hospitals. A team of fourteen, including an experienced sales force, supplying cardiovascular, neuro-surgery & spine, orthopedic & trauma, general surgery, plastic surgery and ENT products.
